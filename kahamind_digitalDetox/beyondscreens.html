Beyond Screens

Beyond Screens is a digital wellness initiative dedicated to helping individuals regain control over their screen time and develop healthier digital habits. Our approach includes personalized assessments, therapy sessions, and a dedicated Care Line for support. Explore the details below to take the first step toward a more balanced digital life.

The Framework
 

The Care Line

Trained professionals offer immediate support, guidance, and crisis intervention.

 

Tech Addiction Screening

Involves administering standardized screening tools and conducting assessments to evaluate participants' digital habits, psychological well-being, and overall functioning.

 

Therapy Sessions

Individual therapy sessions provide a confidential and supportive environment for clients to explore and address underlying psychological issues thus develop coping strategies.

Family Therapy Sessions

A platform for open communication, understanding the impact of technology addiction on family dynamics, and developing strategies to strengthen family support networks.

Referrals for Psychiatric Consultations

Assess participants' mental health status, including screening for mood disorders, anxiety disorders, impulse control disorders, and other psychiatric conditions.

 

Digital Users Support Group

Offers a safe space for members to connect and receive support from peers and professionals through weekly online meetings, facilitated by a psychologist.

 

Group Workshops

Address specific areas of concern or challenges related to digital detox, such as managing screen time, coping with stress, or fostering healthier digital habits.

Limited Digital Use Tracking Programme

A personalized programme that starts with a 48-hour period of app-based tracking to gain insights into digital habits. Followed by a 7-day programme designed to gradually decrease screen time and promote mindful technology use.

Education Videos

Accessible and engaging content on topics such as digital wellness, mindful technology use, and practical strategies for managing screen time.

 

Webinars

Expert speakers who share their knowledge and expertise on topics related to digital wellness, technology use, and strategies for achieving balance.

 

Guest Speaker Sessions

Guest speaker sessions feature presentations and talks by experts in digital wellness, psychology, and related fields.
