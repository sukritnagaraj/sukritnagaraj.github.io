About Us

Beyond Screens is dedicated to helping individuals regain balance, focus, and control over their digital lives. Empowering individuals to foster mindful connections with technology for a healthier future.

This initiative is made possible through the partnership of the Government of Karnataka and AIGF, recognizing the urgent need for digital well-being in an age of constant connectivity. It is supported by Kaha Mind, which aims to bring evidence-based strategies and accessible resources to individuals and communities.

 

Government of Karnataka

The Government of Karnataka recognizes the evolving challenges of the digital age and is taking a forward-thinking approach to address them. Beyond Screens is a first-of-its-kind initiative in the state, dedicated to tackling the growing issue of technology overuse. By raising awareness, providing specialized support, and fostering community engagement, this program empowers individuals to build a healthier, more balanced relationship with the digital world.

All India Gaming Federation (AIGF)

Starting as a policy-focused group, the All India Gaming Federation (AIGF) has evolved into India’s largest gaming federation, representing over 100 member companies and game developers who collectively engage with more than 40 crore Digital Nagriks. As digitalization accelerates, gaming has emerged as a vital force in India’s digital economy. AIGF works closely with stakeholders to promote a responsible, inclusive, and thriving gaming ecosystem—one that not only drives innovation and economic growth but also prioritizes player well-being and industry best practices.

Kaha Mind

Kaha Mind Healthcare Pvt. Ltd., established in 2018 and headquartered in Bangalore, with operational teams in both Bangalore and Delhi, is a leading mental health organization dedicated to providing comprehensive, full-stack mental health services. From therapy and psychoeducational programs to research and content development, Kaha Mind is committed to making mental healthcare more accessible and effective. As the strategic and implementation partner for Beyond Screens, Kaha Mind brings its expertise to support this initiative, ensuring impactful interventions that promote digital well-being and mental health.
