About Us

Beyond Screens is dedicated to helping individuals regain balance, focus, and control over their digital lives. Empowering individuals to foster mindful connections with technology for a healthier future.

This initiative is made possible through Kaha Mind's partnership with the Government of Karnataka and AIGF recognizing the urgent need for digital well-being in an age of constant connectivity. We aim to bring evidence-based strategies and accessible resources to individuals and communities.

Partners
 

Government of Karnataka

The Government of Karnataka's 'Beyond Screens' initiative takes a comprehensive approach to addressing the growing concerns around technology overuse. By raising awareness, providing support services, and bringing communities together, the program aims to enable healthier relationships with the digital world.

 

All India Gaming Federation (AIGF)

Starting as a policy-focused group, AIGF has grown into the nation's largest federation, representing over 100 member companies and game developers who engage with more than 40 crore Digital Nagriks. With digitalization on the rise, gaming has become a key player in India's digital economy. AIGF collaborates closely with stakeholders to foster a responsible, inclusive, and thriving gaming ecosystem that contributes to the nation's growth.

 

Kaha Mind

Kaha Mind Healthcare Pvt Ltd, established in 2018 and headquartered in Bangalore with operational teams in Delhi and Bangalore, is a pioneering mental health organization engaged in full-stack mental health services, including therapy, psychoeducational programs, content, and research.
