Get Involved
Change begins with one step. Whether you're an individual, school, or workplace, you can join the movement for a healthier digital future.
Ways to Join

Individuals

Reach out to our Care Line for support, access free resources, or take the pledge to be more mindful of your screen time. For collaborations or ideas, email us at hello@beyondscreens.net

Schools & Colleges

Empower students with mindful tech habits! Partner with us for workshops, toolkits, lectures, or a school-wide digital detox challenge to promote healthier screen use.

Community & Events

Bring the digital detox movement to your local community. Partner with us to host workshops, retreats, or awareness events that inspire mindful tech use and real-world connections.

Tech-Smart Parenting

Subscribe to our newsletter and reach out for a parenting guide on setting healthy screen boundaries for children.

Workplaces and Organizations

Encourage digital well-being in your workplace. Host a digital detox challenge, provide mindful tech-use resources for employees, and create a healthier, more focused work environment.

Stay Connected

Follow and engage with us on Instagram, X, Facebook, and LinkedIn to stay updated on our latest initiatives, events, and resources.

Programs & Challenges
Explore engaging ways to reset your relationship with technology through our structured programs and challenges:
30-Day Digital Detox Challenge: Small daily actions to help you gradually unplug and build healthier habits.
Tech-Smart Parenting: A guide for parents to set healthy screen boundaries for kids.
Workplace Wellness: Strategies to improve focus, productivity, and work-life balance in a digital-first world.
Screen-Free Saturdays: A community challenge encouraging one day of mindful disconnection each week.
