FAQs

+ What is digital addiction?
Digital addiction, also known as internet addiction disorder (IAD), refers to the compulsive and excessive use of digital devices such as smartphones, computers, or gaming consoles, to the extent that it interferes with daily life. This behavior can negatively impact personal relationships, work, and health.
